# Research Contribution

This model provides:

1. Carrier-dynamics-based analysis of SOA gain and saturation behaviour.

2. Analytical SOA–EDFA crossover criterion based on the balance between noise figure penalty and scintillation suppression benefit.

3. Turbulence-dependent amplifier selection framework for free-space optical communication systems.

4. Numerical validation using Gamma-Gamma atmospheric turbulence modelling and FSO channel simulations.
