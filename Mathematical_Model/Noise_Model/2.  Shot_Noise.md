# Shot Noise Model

## Overview

Shot noise arises from the discrete nature of photon arrival at the photodetector.

It becomes significant in optical receivers operating at low received power levels.

---

# Shot Noise Current

The shot noise variance is:

\[
i_{shot}^{2}=2qI_{pd}B
\]

where:

- \(q\) is electron charge.
- \(I_{pd}\) is photodetector current.
- \(B\) is electrical bandwidth.

The photocurrent is:

\[
I_{pd}=RP_r
\]

where:

- \(R\) is detector responsivity.
- \(P_r\) is received optical power.

---

# Importance in FSO Systems

In turbulent FSO links, received optical power fluctuates due to:

\[
P_r=P_t h
\]

where \(h\) is the Gamma-Gamma turbulence coefficient.

Therefore, turbulence increases shot noise variation indirectly through received power fluctuations.
