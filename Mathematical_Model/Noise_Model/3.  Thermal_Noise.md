# Thermal Noise Model

## Overview

Thermal noise is generated by random electron motion in receiver electronic components.

The thermal noise contribution is independent of optical signal power.

---

# Thermal Noise Power

The thermal noise current is:

\[
i_{thermal}^{2}
=
\frac{4k_BT}{R_L}B
\]

where:

- \(k_B\) is Boltzmann constant.
- \(T\) is temperature.
- \(R_L\) is load resistance.
- \(B\) is electrical bandwidth.

---

# Receiver Noise Contribution

The total receiver noise is:

\[
N_{total}
=
N_{ASE}
+
N_{shot}
+
N_{thermal}
\]

This total noise is used for SNR calculation.
