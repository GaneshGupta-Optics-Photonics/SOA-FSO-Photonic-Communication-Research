## Abstract

This work reviews semiconductor optical amplifiers (SOAs), focusing on carrier dynamics, gain saturation effects, gain recovery behaviour, and their role in advanced photonic communication systems.

The study highlights computational modelling approaches and performance considerations for high-speed optical communication networks.

---

## Research Areas

- Semiconductor Optical Amplifiers
- Carrier Dynamics
- Gain Saturation
- Gain Recovery
- Photonic Communication Systems
- Optical Communication
- Computational Modelling

---

## Research Significance

This publication establishes the foundation of the research programme by examining the physical mechanisms and performance limitations of semiconductor optical amplifiers in advanced optical communication systems.

The work provides a basis for subsequent investigations into SOA gain saturation, free-space optical communication, atmospheric turbulence, and adaptive optical amplification.
