## Overview

This review examines semiconductor optical amplifiers (SOAs) for next-generation optical communication systems, with emphasis on material systems, device physics, gain saturation, noise, and photonic integration.

The study reviews fundamental SOA mechanisms including carrier population inversion, stimulated emission, carrier dynamics, and gain saturation. Bulk, quantum-well, and quantum-dot SOA technologies are discussed together with emerging approaches for high-capacity optical communication systems.

Recent developments in tapered waveguide structures, heterogeneous integration, silicon photonics, advanced modulation formats, and adaptive SOA control are also considered.

## Research Significance

The review establishes the physical and technological foundation for subsequent research into SOA-based optical communication systems, including free-space optical communication, atmospheric turbulence mitigation, and adaptive SOA control.
