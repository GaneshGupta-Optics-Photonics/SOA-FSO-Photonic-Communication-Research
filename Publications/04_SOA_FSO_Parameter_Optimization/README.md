## Abstract

This study presents a numerical investigation of parameter optimization in gain-saturated semiconductor optical amplifiers (SOAs) for scintillation suppression in free-space optical communication links.

A rate-equation model of an SOA operating in the saturation regime is coupled with a Gamma-Gamma atmospheric turbulence channel model. The effects of bias current, active-region length, and input optical coupling efficiency are systematically investigated.

The study evaluates scintillation-index reduction, BER improvement, power penalty, and ASE-noise limitations.

The results identify an optimized operating region with a bias current of approximately 150 mA, active-region length of approximately 350 μm, and input coupling efficiency of 0.8 under the investigated conditions.

The optimized configuration achieves up to 6.2 dB reduction in scintillation index and reduces the power penalty required for BER = 10⁻³ from 8.5 dB to 3.1 dB under strong turbulence.
